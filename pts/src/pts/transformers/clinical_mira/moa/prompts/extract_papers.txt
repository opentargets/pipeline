
PUBLICATIONS:
- Some entries are PubMed abstracts of publications that report these trials. Their "Trial ID" is "pmid" followed by the PubMed id (e.g. "pmid39167366").
- Treat them exactly like trials: the TEXT ONLY rule and every quote rule apply. Cite the publication's id, as written after "Trial ID:", as the nctId of its quotes.
- Only what a publication says about the drug in the input counts. Statements about comparators, background therapy or other drugs do not.
